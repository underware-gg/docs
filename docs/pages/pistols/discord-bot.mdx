# Discord Bot

Pistols at Dawn duels, playable straight inside a Discord channel: no wallet, no install, no leaving the conversation. You pick your moves through the bot's menus and watch the result play out as an ASCII animation. Challenges and results post publicly by default, because duels are meant to be watched, not hidden away in a DM.

This page covers what the bot is and how its duels resolve. For setup, play instructions, and commands, jump straight to the pages below.

## Classic Mode

The bot runs its own rule set, called Classic Mode. Every duelist starts with 3 health, and a duel walks through ten paces: each side secretly chooses a pace to fire on, a pace to dodge on, one tactics card, and one blades card. Land your dodge on the exact pace your opponent fires and the shot is negated; otherwise a hit chance roll decides whether it lands.

Classic Mode plays by the same rules as the onchain game: the same cards, 34-card Environment deck, starting stats, dodges, blades matchups, and winning conditions. Both versions begin with 3 health, a flat 50% base hit chance at every pace, and 1 base damage. A dodge only works on the exact pace your opponent fires. If you know how to play one version, you know how to play the other.

What differs sits outside the duel itself. Bot duels do not touch your wallet, stake FAME, or affect onchain seasons and rankings. Your choices remain private from your opponent, but Discord and the bot protect them and roll the cards and shots rather than the onchain game proving everything publicly. Same game, lighter guarantees.

## How It Works

Each player picks their four cards through a private menu that only they can see. The bot keeps those choices hidden from the opponent until both sides have locked in, then decides the full duel using its own random shuffle and hit rolls. Nothing is decided later.

The animation shows that already-decided duel step by step; it does not reroll or change the outcome. Active duels, player stats, queues, and server settings survive a bot restart.
