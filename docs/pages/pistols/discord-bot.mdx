# Discord Bot

Pistols at Dawn, playable inside Discord: challenge another player to a Classic duel, practise against an Imp, or take a solo run through [the Gauntlet](/pistols/discord-gauntlet). No wallet, install or trip out of the conversation required.

Player duels use private move controls and public results. Targeted invitations live in your inbox and try private delivery first; open challenges, public callouts and accepted matches give the rest of the channel something to watch. Gauntlet runs have their own public adventure card, with private inventory and character controls.

## Pick Your Fight

- **Another player:** `/duel user opponent:@someone` opens the challenge setup. You can answer and complete different invitations independently through `/pistols inbox`.
- **An open challenge:** `/duel public` invites the channel; the first valid acceptance takes the duel.
- **An Imp:** `/duel bot` opens a private practice-opponent picker. Browse the cards and choose **Duel this Imp** when you're ready.
- **The road:** `/pistols gauntlet start` previews a solo adventure. Confirm to take a seat at the Fool & Flintlock, or locate your existing run.

Start with `/pistols tutorial` for private onboarding, or `/pistols menu` for guidance and shortcuts. Operators can enable an optional three-lesson story tutorial; its NPC encounters are scripted teaching exercises, while ordinary practice uses normal Classic rolls.

See [For Players](/pistols/discord-players) for Classic duels, [The Gauntlet](/pistols/discord-gauntlet) for solo adventures, [For Server Admins](/pistols/discord-admins) for setup, and the [Command Reference](/pistols/discord-commands) for exact syntax.

## Classic Duels

Each duelist starts with 3 health and privately chooses a fire pace, a dodge pace, one tactics card and one blades card. The pistol exchange runs through ten paces. Base hit chance is 50% and base damage is 1, before cards and the Environment change the odds. A dodge cancels a shot only when it matches the opponent's exact fire pace; surviving duelists may settle the matter with blades.

You can pick all four choices manually or let an unlocked pistol archetype choose them. Your opponent cannot see your committed hand before resolution. An unscheduled duel resolves once it is accepted and both players have locked their moves. A scheduled public callout waits for its settlement time, even if both hands are ready earlier.

The animation plays the saved result step by step; watching or replaying it does not reroll the fight. The first animation is public. **Replay** and **Combat Log** open privately and remain available while the saved combat result exists.

Discord duels do not stake wallet FAME or affect onchain seasons and rankings. Discord and the bot protect your choices and perform the random rolls; these are separate from the onchain game's public verification.

## The Gauntlet

The Gauntlet is a solo adventure through road events, shops and thirteen opponent encounters, ending with a boss. Pick your actions each exchange, manage equipment and supplies, and decide when to risk another fight or take an offered way home. Its **Shoot**, **Steady Aim**, **Taunt**, **Dodge** and **Counter** controls are separate from Classic's four-card duel picker.

Runs, inventory and progress survive restarts. You have one active run across servers, tied to its original channel. Gauntlet coins and Fame are adventure scores, separate from wallet tokens and Classic records.

Gauntlet shipped in bot v0.4.0. The [Gauntlet guide](/pistols/discord-gauntlet) also marks the upcoming refinements, including injuries, pistol improvements and new next-run controls; availability depends on your server's bot version.

## Saved Progress

Classic invitations, accepted duels and unfinished choices survive bot restarts. Use `/pistols inbox` to find outstanding matches, including invitations whose notification could not be delivered. Classic has no automatic inactivity expiry; a scheduled callout's settlement deadline still applies.

Gauntlet has a separate fourteen-day inactivity limit. A successful gameplay action renews it; looking at status or inventory does not. See [Saving and Resuming](/pistols/discord-gauntlet#saving-and-resuming) for the channel and recovery rules.
