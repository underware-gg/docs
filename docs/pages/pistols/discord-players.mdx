# For Players

Someone in your server insulted your honour. Or your cooking. Or your taste in music. There is exactly one civilised response: pistols at dawn. This page covers Classic duels with other players and the practice Imps, from your first challenge to reading your win-loss record.

You do not need a wallet or a crypto account. Start in a server with the bot installed; [Before You Start](/pistols/discord-bot#before-you-start) explains how to use its slash commands. For quick access, `/duel user opponent:@someone`, `/duel public`, and `/duel bot` work exactly like their `/pistols duel` equivalents. Both paths share the same cooldown and server/channel restrictions.

Want a solo adventure instead? Start with the [Gauntlet guide](/pistols/discord-gauntlet). Its equipment, coins and Fame belong to that mode; the four-card duels below are separate.

## Your First Duel

Start with `/pistols tutorial`. By default, it opens a private welcome, a **Full rules** toggle, and a **Try it against the practice bot** button. Or run `/pistols menu` for private shortcuts to duel guidance, stats, history, inbox, balances, status, help, tutorial and Gauntlet guidance.

For a warm-up, run `/pistols duel bot` in a server, browse the Imp cards and choose **Duel this Imp**. An Imp is a computer-controlled practice opponent. It locks its moves immediately; you choose and confirm yours through **Duelling Grounds**, the bot's duel-control panel. If the server has a dedicated duel channel, start there. See [Practising with Imps](#practising-with-imps) for the roster and rewards.

Ready to challenge a person?

1. In the server's duel channel (or any usable channel if none is configured), run `/pistols duel user opponent:@someone`.
2. Choose a themed premise in the private picker, or choose Custom and submit its text form. The invitation enters both players' duel inboxes.
3. You can choose **Pick Your Moves** immediately. Your opponent opens the invitation through its notification or `/pistols inbox`, then accepts and chooses their moves.
4. Choose a pistol archetype to generate your four choices, or choose Manual and pick fire, dodge, tactics and blades yourself.
5. Confirm your moves. An accepted, unscheduled duel resolves automatically once both players have committed. A [scheduled callout](#callouts-and-settlement) waits until its settlement time.
6. With animation enabled, either duelist can choose **Watch Duel** once the saved result is ready, or wait about 15 seconds for it to play automatically. With animation disabled, the result posts directly.

Your four choices are your **hand**. To **commit** a hand means to confirm and lock those choices; you cannot change them afterwards. An automatic pistol archetype fills in the choices for you, but you still confirm them. [Choosing Your Cards](#choosing-your-cards) explains each choice.

Use `/pistols duel public` if you would rather invite the channel to take its best shot. The first valid acceptance takes that open challenge.

## Practising with Imps

`/pistols duel bot` and `/duel bot` open a private picker starting on **Nasty Imp**. Browse with **Previous** and **Next**, then choose **Duel this Imp** to start the public duel. There is no premise picker or acceptance step.

| Imp | How they play |
| --- | --- |
| Nasty Imp | Villainous: favours early shots. |
| Mischievous Imp | Trickster: unpredictable choices. |
| Kind Imp | Honourable: favours later shots. |
| Veteran Imp | Chooses a playing archetype for each duel. Unlocks after your first victory over any Imp. |

The tutorial's practice button chooses an Imp at random from Nasty, Mischievous and Kind, adding Veteran once you have beaten an Imp.

Practice uses normal Classic rolls and does not affect your combat win/loss record. Finishing a practice duel still advances normal pistol unlocks and qualifies you for the Duelist badge. Beating an Imp records its trophy under `/pistols stats`; collect all four across any servers where you play. The duel, animation and result are public even though the opponent picker and your moves are private.

## Challenging Someone

There are two ways to start a duel with `/pistols duel`:

- **Targeted**: `/pistols duel user opponent:@someone` invites a specific person. Only they can accept or reject it.
- **Open**: `/pistols duel public` posts to the channel without a named opponent. Anyone eligible except you can accept; first valid acceptance wins.

Either way, you're first shown a private picker asking for a **premise**: the reason for the duel. Choose from 14 named options: Honour, Debt, Dispute, Hatred, Vengeance, Blood, Nothing, Insult, Matter, Lesson, Affection, Pride, Wager and Mischief; or pick Custom and write your own, up to 80 characters. Themed reasons need no extra form. Your private confirmation offers **State your grievance** to add optional detail while the challenge awaits a response. Enter the words that complete the displayed premise; a blank submission changes nothing. Custom requires its text form before the challenge is created.

A targeted invitation enters both players' inboxes. Notification delivery tries the server's enabled routes in order: a DM, a private thread, then a public challenge. Your opponent can always find the invitation with `/pistols inbox`, even if notification delivery fails. Private notification threads are visible to their recipient, the bot and moderators; the recipient must be able to see the parent channel.

An open challenge is public from the start. You can have one unanswered open challenge per server. Another unfinished duel does not prevent you from starting or accepting a different Classic matchup, but there can only be one outstanding duel between the same two players in that server, in either direction. A duplicate targeted challenge opens the existing duel instead of creating another.

You can deliberately announce a targeted invitation with **Call Them Out**. Acceptance also makes the agreed matchup public. Your premise picker, move picker and locked choices stay private.

## Being Challenged

Open the invitation from its notification or `/pistols inbox` to see your private Duelling Grounds controls.

- **Accept** agrees to the duel. The bot announces the accepted matchup in the channel and retires an earlier public challenge or callout. Choose **Pick Your Moves** to prepare your hand.
- **Reject** cancels the invitation and publicly shames you for refusing satisfaction. This is a social outcome, not a combat loss.
- **Leave it unanswered** and an unscheduled invitation stays in your inbox. A scheduled callout has a deadline even if you never accept it, so read its settlement time before walking away.

You can answer or finish invitations independently while other duels remain unfinished. For an open challenge, use **To the Duelling Grounds** on the public post to accept. If two people try at once, only the first valid acceptance succeeds.

The public Duelling Grounds button is useful for spectators too. It shows the duel's status, premise and mode, with actions to challenge either participant. Private invitation controls are available only to the two players involved.

## Your Duel Inbox

`/pistols inbox` lists your outstanding invitations and accepted duels, oldest first. Open any entry to respond, choose moves or withdraw. Use **Previous** and **Next** for more entries. `/pistols queue` opens this same inbox; you do not have to wait for an earlier duel to finish.

The challenger can lock in moves before the invitation is accepted. The opponent accepts first, then picks their hand. Draft choices and committed moves survive bot restarts; drafts have no separate inactivity expiry. Once confirmed, stale controls cannot overwrite locked moves.

Only one outstanding duel per pair is allowed in a server. Finish or close it before starting another challenge against that same opponent there. Your other matchups can continue independently.

## Callouts and Settlement

On an unanswered targeted invitation, the challenger can choose **Call Them Out** once to post the grievance publicly. **Settlement** is the appointed time when the bot checks readiness and resolves or cancels that duel. Leave the settlement field blank for an unscheduled callout, or set a date and time with an explicit UTC offset, for example `2026-11-20T20:00+11:00`: 8 pm on 20 November 2026 at UTC+11. Replace it with your intended future date, time and offset; the example does not mean the server's local time. The time must be at least 24 hours after you issue the callout and cannot be changed once posted.

A scheduled callout binds the recipient immediately. They must both **accept and confirm their moves before settlement**. The challenger must have confirmed their moves too. When both are ready, the duel waits for the appointed time, then resolves. Saved appointments and moves survive restarts; the bot processes an overdue settlement when it recovers.

At the deadline, missing acceptance or moves cancels the duel and publicly shames every unready participant. If both players fail, neither has a winning outcome. A late click cannot rescue the challenge. Either player can also withdraw from an accepted duel and face public shame. Withdrawing an unannounced, unscheduled invitation stays private.

Rejection, public withdrawal and missed settlement are social outcomes. They do not award combat wins or pistol unlocks and do not change your combat win/loss record.

## Spectator Predictions

On a public callout or accepted duel between players, spectators can back either duelist or predict a draw. Each spectator has one prediction and can change it until the duel closes or settlement begins. You cannot predict your own match.

Results show how people voted. Backers share the shame if their chosen duelist chickens out. These are social predictions: no FAME or FOOLS is spent or transferred.

## Timeouts

Classic invitations, open challenges, practice duels and accepted duels have **no automatic inactivity expiry**. The old accept window and busy-player queue timers do not apply. A settlement time deliberately set through **Call Them Out** is the deadline you need to watch.

The Gauntlet has its own inactivity rules; see the [Gauntlet guide](/pistols/discord-gauntlet).

## Choosing Your Cards

Open **Pick Your Moves** in your private invitation or Duelling Grounds controls. As the challenger, you can do this before acceptance; as the opponent, accept first. Nobody, including your opponent and spectators, can see your picks while you prepare them.

You're choosing four cards in total:

- **Fire**: the pace (1 through 10) at which you pull the trigger.
- **Dodge**: the pace at which you duck. This is the bit that trips people up: a dodge only cancels an incoming shot if it exactly matches the pace your opponent fired at. Picking the same pace for both isn't allowed; in Manual mode you'll get a warning and be asked to repick.
- **Tactics**: a card that shapes your stats for the whole duel.
- **Blades**: a card that modifies pistol stats and determines your move in the melee phase if both duelists survive the shots.

Each duelist starts with 3 health. The pistol exchange advances through up to ten **paces**, revealing an **Environment** card at each pace. These cards can change hit chance, damage or tactics effects before you fire; choosing a later pace does not by itself guarantee a more accurate shot. If both duelists survive their shots, their Blades cards resolve close combat. The [Strategy Guide](/pistols/strategy-guide) explains the individual Tactics and Blades cards.

You have two ways to fill these in:

**Auto-play archetypes.** Instead of picking cards yourself, you can tap a pistol archetype and let it choose all four cards for you using a fixed personality. The choices are:

- **🎩 Honourable**: patient. Fires at paces 6 to 10, weighted toward the later paces, and plays for grit and precision over speed.
- **🃏 Trickster**: unpredictable. Can fire at any pace, with a preference for the middle, and favours the chaos tactics.
- **🩸 Villainous**: aggressive. Fires at paces 1 to 5, weighted toward the earliest paces; shoots first and asks questions later.
- **Manual**: the four dropdowns described above, for full control.

By default, you start with **Honourable** and **Manual** unlocked; the bot operator can configure a different starter archetype. **Trickster** unlocks the first time you finish a duel, win, lose, or draw; it doesn't matter which. **Villainous** unlocks after your second finished duel, same rule. A duel you reject, withdraw from or forfeit at settlement does not count toward either: only a duel that actually resolves does.

Whichever way you fill your four picks, they stay hidden from your opponent until the resolved duel reveals them. Confirming locks your hand; it does not expose it early in a scheduled duel.

## Watching the Duel

An accepted, unscheduled duel resolves when both players have confirmed their moves. A scheduled duel waits until settlement. The bot saves the result before presenting it: the animation shows what happened and never rerolls the outcome.

With animation enabled, either duelist can choose **Watch Duel** to start the public animation early. If nobody does, it starts automatically after about 15 seconds. With animation disabled, or if the animation cannot be built, the bot posts the saved result directly.

The result names the winner or draw, gives a conclusion tied to the premise and actual finish, repeats the full premise, and shows the key moments and remaining health.

Below it you'll find:

- **Combat Log**: a private, detailed breakdown with a perspective appropriate to the viewer. It remains available while the saved combat result exists, with no access timeout.
- **Replay**: the animation in a separate private message for you, leaving the public result untouched. It has no access timeout while the saved combat result is usable. Missing or unusable data produces **Replay expired**.
- **Challenge**: a fresh challenge against the human winner, or either human player after a draw. A practice result targets the human participant rather than the Imp. This opens the usual premise picker; it is not an instant rematch.
- **Share**: opens an X/Twitter compose window with suggested result text and a link. You choose whether to publish it.

The first play is public; Replay is private. Combat Log and Replay are rebuilt from saved results after a bot restart. Public celebrations may follow a first duel, first Imp victory, comeback, streak or rivalry. Their delivery does not change the recorded result or trophy progress.

### Reading the Animation

The strip below the duelists shows environment cards drawn so far. The current card is marked `>`; `..` marks cards not yet drawn. The line below the canvas gives the exact changes to each duelist's hit chance and damage.

| Strip code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `D+`, `D-`, `D2` | Damage increase, decrease or double increase. |
| `H+`, `H-`, `H2` | Hit-chance increase, decrease or double increase. |
| `**`, `xx` | All Shots Hit, All Shots Miss. |
| `T2`, `T0` | Tactics modifiers doubled or nullified. |

A 🔒 beside hit chance marks an active **All Shots Hit** or **All Shots Miss** override. Later chance cards can have no effect while that override remains active; the log explains why. An ordinary chance cap has no lock and can still change. A perfectly timed dodge can evade even a 100% shot. Older saved duels may give a shorter explanation where that detail was not recorded.

## Your Record

- **`/pistols stats`** shows your combat wins, losses and draws, win rate, current streak, best streak and four-Imp trophy wall. A win extends your streak, a loss resets it, and a draw changes neither. Practice victories add Imp trophies without adding combat wins.
- **`/pistols history`** summarises outstanding duels and your last 10 resolved duels. Use the inbox for the full paginated list of outstanding invitations and accepted matches.

## Finding Players and Browsing Duelists

Use `/pistols players` for a private browser of players in your server. Sort by recent activity, most duels or highest current streak, or search for any server member. Cards show public duel activity, recognition and Imp trophies, with linked portraits when available. Private unanswered invitations do not appear in public activity.

**Challenge** opens the usual premise picker in the configured duel channel. A public active duel may offer **Spectate** instead; a recent Classic result can offer a replay. You can browse or challenge a player who has not linked a wallet.

`/pistols duelists` is a separate private catalogue of 96 duelist cards across Imps, Characters, Genesis, Legends and Pirates. Switch collections and browse pages without starting a duel. To fight an Imp, use `/pistols duel bot`.

## Optional Story Tutorial

If the bot operator enables the story tutorial, `/pistols tutorial` and Tutorial buttons open three scripted NPC lessons instead of the welcome/rules/practice view. The first teaches firing, the second adds dodging, and the third adds tactics and blades. The lessons use scripted scenarios and fixed rolls, with explanations responding to your choices; ordinary practice duels remain random Classic duels.

The first lesson is mandatory before skipping the remainder. Finishing or skipping after that lesson grants the starter pistol and Manual, and qualifies you for Duelist. It does not grant every archetype. Unfinished progress resumes across bot restarts without an inactivity limit, and old buttons cannot change newer progress. Completed lessons remain visible as you continue. The story flow is off by default.

## Underware Badges

A resolved duel, including practice, qualifies you for **Duelist** in the Underware community server. When a duel resolves, the bot attempts to grant it to eligible members of Underware, whichever server hosted the duel. Story tutorial completion or skipping after its mandatory first lesson also qualifies you when that feature is enabled.

Run `/pistols verify` in Underware to recover a failed automatic grant, or claim the badge after joining or rejoining. Outside Underware, it reports eligibility and links to the official server when an invite is configured; it grants no roles in other servers. Badge delivery depends on the bot's role configuration.

Server owners can also qualify for **Guild Owner** in Underware. Installation records the owner's qualification even if they have not joined Underware yet; joining later or verifying there can recover the role.

## Sending Feedback

Use `/pistols feedback category:<bug|suggestion|praise|other>` for a private feedback form, with an optional `attachment:` screenshot or recording on the command. The team receives your authored submission with Discord identity and server context. You can submit up to three times per hour.

If the operator enables the optional attachment fallback, a submission without a file offers **Add a screenshot or recording**. Clicking it captures your next message with an attachment in that channel within five minutes and acknowledges it with ✅. An up-front command attachment works without this fallback. Staff may reply to your feedback by DM when a team inbox is configured; closed DMs can prevent delivery.

## Linking a Wallet

You can link a Cartridge Controller wallet with `/pistols account link username:<your-username>`, and check token balances with `/pistols account balance` (shows LORDS, FOOLS, FAME, and STRK for you or another linked player).

This is entirely optional. Duelling, picking cards, tracking stats and using your inbox all work with zero wallet link. Linking verifies that your Controller username is already connected to this same Discord account in Pistols; it does not create a wallet or an external link. Your own Not Linked balance response also offers a **Link wallet** button.

Use `/pistols account unlink` to remove the bot's local mapping. This does not disconnect your external Cartridge/Pistols identity: balance lookup checks the external Discord-to-wallet link first, so that lookup can still succeed after local unlinking.
