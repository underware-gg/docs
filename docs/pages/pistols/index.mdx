## What a duel is

Two duelists, one dispute. Both sides secretly pick their cards: when to fire, when to dodge, a tactic to shape the fight, a blade to fall back on; then they reveal together. The shots ring out pace by pace as an Environment deck turns the odds. Survive the pistols and it comes down to blades. Someone falls. The chain remembers who.

## Two ways to duel

* **[The Game](/pistols/the-game)**: the full onchain experience. Duelists as NFTs, FAME on the line, seasons and leaderboards, honour permanently etched into the record.
* **[Discord Bot](/pistols/discord-bot)**: settle it right in the channel. No wallet, no install, first shot in seconds.
